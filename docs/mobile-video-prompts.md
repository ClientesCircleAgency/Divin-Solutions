## Home

Ultra-realistic vertical 9:16 video, 10 seconds, bright modern construction office interior. A client in a dark suit and tie enters the office with the owner of Divin Solutions, who wears work trousers, a clean shirt, a white safety helmet and carries rolled construction drawings and site papers. They walk towards a large window overlooking an active construction site with cranes, concrete structure and workers in the distance. Both look out at the project, exchange a confident smile, then shake hands warmly. Natural daylight, premium corporate construction tone, realistic human movement, no text, no logos, no subtitles, no exaggerated acting, stable cinematic camera, gentle forward dolly, realistic Portuguese/European business environment.

## Civil Construction

Bright realistic daylight, European construction site, no text, no logos, no subtitles, stable cinematic tracking shot, natural body language.
